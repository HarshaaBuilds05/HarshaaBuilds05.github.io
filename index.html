My works

Two projects I’m proud of. Click any screenshot to enlarge.

ServiLink: a trusted local service platform

A full-stack MERN marketplace that connects people with electricians, plumbers and cleaners and covers the full booking flow. Customers, providers and admins each get their own dashboard, and every booking moves from pending to completed, followed by a rating and review.

My role: backend. I built the REST API (15+ endpoints, JWT auth), the Emergency Alert feature that shortlists every available provider in real time, and the Trust Score that combines ratings, completed jobs and verified feedback.
